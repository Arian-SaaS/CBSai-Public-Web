AI-native by design

Every module has an agent.
The agents have a network.

Specialist agents work inside the departments they belong to — Ziba in finance, Jupiter in CRM, Atoosa in HR, Jupiter Ops across vendors and inventory, and a project management agent in delivery. Agent Ops carries context between them, so a vendor delay reaches the schedule, the invoice and the margin on its own. Artemis and Artemis Shadow sit at the centre, connected to every dashboard through Agent Ops and learning your workspace from day one. CBSai does not put a chatbot beside your business; it builds intelligence into the workflows that run it — with a human approving anything consequential.

Fit & commercials

Worth knowing before
you book anything.

Qualifying yourself out quickly is more useful than a discovery call that goes nowhere. Here is where CBSai fits today, where it does not, and how we price.

Strong fit
  • Operations-heavy businesses where a delay in one department shows up as margin loss in another — construction, field services, install and maintenance contractors, specialist trades
  • Roughly 25–500 people, running projects with materials, subcontractors and labour against a budget
  • Teams currently reconciling finance, delivery and supply by spreadsheet and memory
  • A leader who can name the single workflow costing them the most money
Not a fit yet
  • Businesses wanting a drop-in replacement for a working, deeply customised ERP
  • Organisations needing a completed compliance certification pack today — we will not claim certifications we do not hold
  • Teams looking for a standalone chatbot to sit beside existing tools
  • Under ~15 people, where the coordination cost this solves has not appeared yet
How we price

Per-workspace subscription with a per-seat component, scaled to the modules you actually turn on. You are not charged for departments you have not adopted. We publish a firm number after a walkthrough, because quoting before understanding your operation produces a figure neither of us can stand behind.
