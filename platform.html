How the AI actually works

Every module has an agent.
The agents have a network.

Most platforms attach an assistant to a screen. CBSai runs three connected layers, because the expensive problems in an operations business happen between departments, not inside them.

TIER 01

Module agents

Specialist agents working inside the tasks your team already runs. They draft, check, reconcile and flag — reducing the manual steps where mistakes usually enter. Some span more than one department: Jupiter Ops carries vendors and inventory together, because supply and stock move as one problem.

Ziba · Jupiter · Atoosa · Jupiter Ops · Project Management Agent
TIER 02

Agent Ops

The bridge between departments. When a supplier slips, Agent Ops carries that fact into the schedule, the labour plan, the invoice and the margin — without anyone re-entering it or noticing too late.

Cross-department context
TIER 03

Artemis & Artemis Shadow

Artemis is the central brain, connected to every dashboard through Agent Ops. Artemis Shadow learns your workspace from day one — your records, your workflows, your decisions — so the intelligence fits how your business runs, not a generic industry average.
