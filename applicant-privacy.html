Applicant Privacy Information

This information applies when you send CBSai an expression of interest, résumé, portfolio, or other information about a possible future role through www.cbsai.info or by email.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Planned roles

The roles shown on this website are planned roles and expressions of interest, not guarantees that a role is currently open or that an employment relationship will be offered.

2. Information you may share

This may include your name, contact details, résumé, portfolio, work history, skills, location, availability, compensation expectations, and anything else you choose to include. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is not relevant to your interest.

3. How we use it

We use applicant information to review expressions of interest, communicate about potential opportunities, understand future hiring needs, and protect the integrity of the process. We do not use applicant information to make solely automated hiring decisions.

4. Access and sharing

Information may be reviewed by people involved in planning or evaluating future CBSai roles and by service providers that help us securely manage business communications. We do not sell applicant information or use it for advertising.

5. Retention and your choices

We retain applicant information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above and applicable recordkeeping. You may ask us to correct or delete information, or withdraw your expression of interest, by emailing marketing@cbsai.co with the subject “Applicant privacy request.”

6. Security and updates

We use reasonable safeguards for information received through the public website and email. No transmission method is completely secure. We may update this information as our hiring process becomes formalized; the date above identifies the current version.

7. Related policy

For information about the public website, cookies, and demo or newsletter submissions, read the Privacy Policy.
