A practical adoption path

Do not replace everything on day one.

Start around a workflow where the context is already expensive to reconstruct. Configure the workspace, bring in relevant data, establish approvals, then expand when the operating model is ready.

  1. 01UnderstandMap teams, workflows, and systems.
  2. 02ConfigureSet up roles, modules, and structure.
  3. 03ConnectBring in the information that matters.
  4. 04ExpandExtend context when the core is ready.
