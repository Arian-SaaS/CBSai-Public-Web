Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CBSai handles information collected through www.cbsai.info, the public marketing website. The product application at cbsai.co is a separate service and may have additional terms and privacy information.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you request a demo, we collect the work email, company name, company size, and area of interest that you choose to submit. When you subscribe to updates, we collect your email address. If you contact us directly, we receive the information included in your message.

The website also stores your privacy choice. Optional analytics are not activated unless you explicitly allow them. If enabled, the site may send basic visit and interaction events to a first-party analytics endpoint configured by CBSai; it does not send form contents to analytics.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to requests, provide requested updates, understand interest in CBSai, protect the website, and maintain records needed to manage communications. We do not sell personal information or use submitted lead details for unrelated advertising.

3. Cookies and local storage

Necessary storage remembers your privacy preferences and supports core website behavior. Optional analytics storage and events are disabled by default and remain disabled unless you choose to allow analytics through the privacy controls. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy choices in the site footer.

4. Service providers

We may use hosting, email delivery, form delivery, analytics, and security providers to operate this website and respond to you. They may process information only to provide services to CBSai and according to their own applicable terms. We do not add advertising pixels or marketing tag managers to this public site.

5. Retention and security

We retain communications for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use access controls, encrypted transport, and security measures appropriate to this website. No internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may unsubscribe from optional updates, withdraw analytics consent, or ask us to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. Contact marketing@cbsai.co with the subject “Privacy request.”

7. Children and third-party sites

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children. Links to other websites, including the CBSai product domain, are governed by those sites’ own policies.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Questions about this policy can be sent to marketing@cbsai.co.
